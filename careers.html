Sales & Business Development

Business Development Manager

London, UK

Job Description

This is a rewarding position to maximise your earning potential by selling our services to small and medium-sized businesses. Experience in sales is a must, as well as a desire to be successful and self-motivated.

We offer uncapped earnings along with full training and ongoing support. Fast-track promotion is available for the right and deserving people.

What you will benefit from:
  • Generous bonus and commission package.
  • Full marketing support and training.
  • Ongoing support from an experienced team.
  • Opportunity to progress to Area Sales Manager.

Area Sales Manager

London, UK

Job Description

This is a self-employed role managing existing or new teams of self-employed sales people throughout your area. Working for a customer-friendly business solutions provider, you will be selling our range of products to new and existing users, from SMEs to corporate clients.
