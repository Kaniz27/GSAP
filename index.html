Business water was deregulated in England in April 2017, giving businesses the ability to choose their water supplier. While the market is still developing, this change allows small and medium-sized businesses to explore alternative providers and compare options. Significant savings may take time to emerge, but switching suppliers can help businesses start managing costs more effectively and prepare for future opportunities in the deregulated market.
