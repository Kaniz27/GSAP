It’s a common alternative name for a card machine.

There are lots of different names for them, but they all allow you to take card payments. With a PDQ machine, you’ll be able to offer your customers an alternative way to pay, plus reduce cash handling within your business. You’ll also benefit from driving in customer footfall and more impulse purchases.

A merchant account is a business bank account which is used to acquire payments from your clients’ debit and credit cards. The charges are taken from your client’s account and sent directly to your merchant account, where the funds are held until they are settled into your nominated bank account.

Merchant accounts will:

  • Support you and your customers with the best security & data protection.
  • Guarantee debit and credit card transactions are processed safely and securely.
  • Protect you against chargebacks.
  • Pre-check customer’s accounts for sufficient funds before payment authorisation.

At Total Processing we can provision an online merchant account, sometimes referred to as a MiD or merchant ID, for your business. D&A Business have built up strong partnerships with a number of merchant ISOs and service providers, and can help to find the best merchant account and service for your needs.

How do they Work?

  • An EPOS is a modern day till system, making it easy to process transactions and collect data on each payment.
  • It gives you complete visibility and control of your business, your inventory and your staff.
  • You can log into this remotely, so you’ll always be able to see how your business is performing, wherever in the world you are.

What is an EPOS system?

  • This is called an electronic point of sale system (EPOS). It makes it easier to take payments and manage your business day-to-day, including sales reporting, stock control, accounting and staff management.
  • The system uses up-to-the-minute technology and can be customised to your business’ needs. It can work independently, or be integrated with your current devices.
  • Their reliability and efficiency makes them a popular choice with businesses all over the world.

Payment Services

Everything you need to know

Find answers to common questions about our payment services, PDQ machines and business payment solutions.

01

What payment services do you provide?

We provide a range of payment solutions including card machines, online payments, EPOS systems and secure payment services designed for UK businesses.

Our team can help you find a payment solution that matches your business type, transaction volume and customer requirements.

02

What is a PDQ machine?

A PDQ machine is a card payment terminal that allows your customers to pay using debit cards, credit cards and supported contactless payment methods.

Modern card machines make it easier for businesses to accept payments quickly, securely and conveniently.

03

Can you help reduce my payment processing costs?

Yes. We can review your current requirements and help identify suitable payment solutions and competitive processing options for your business.

Our goal is to help you choose a payment solution that provides value while meeting your business needs.

04

Are PDQ machines suitable for small businesses?

Absolutely. Payment solutions can be selected based on the size, type and individual requirements of your business.

Whether you operate a small shop, restaurant, office or service business, we can help you explore suitable options.

05

Can I accept contactless and mobile payments?

Many modern payment terminals support contactless and mobile payment methods, helping businesses provide customers with convenient ways to pay.

Available payment methods can vary depending on the selected terminal and payment provider.

06

How can I get a payment service quote?

Simply contact our team and tell us about your business requirements. We will help you explore suitable payment options.
